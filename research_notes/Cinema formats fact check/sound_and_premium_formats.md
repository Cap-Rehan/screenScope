# Cinema sound systems and premium-format comparisons

Label key: [MFR] manufacturer claim, [IND] independent, [SEC] regulatory filing, [SEC-WIKI] Wikipedia (secondary, cites others), [EST] estimate, [UNRESOLVED].
Method note: ~12 tool calls. imax.com returned 403 and web.archive.org is blocked in this environment, so no IMAX marketing page was read directly. No HDTVTest, in70mm.com, American Cinematographer or Cinema Technology page was retrieved. Nothing below is from instrument measurements except where stated.

## (a) Sound: IMAX vs Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, Sonics, Auro, 5.1/7.1

### Takeaway
IMAX uses its own proprietary speaker layouts (about 6 channels on legacy systems, 12 on IMAX with Laser). Dolby documents Atmos as a 9.1 bed + up to 118 objects, up to 64 discrete speaker feeds. I found no primary IMAX statement either confirming or denying Atmos in IMAX theatres; the 10-K says only "proprietary" sound.

### Cited Findings
- IMAX FY2025 10-K: "IMAX sound systems use proprietary loudspeaker systems and proprietary surround sound configurations"; systems include "a digital audio system"; the Competition section refers to "the IMAX immersive sound system"; ~50% of materials in each sound system are proprietary parts. [SEC] — [IMAX 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/921582/000162828026011770/imax-20251231.htm), Item 1 (IMAX Film Remastering; Sound System Component Manufacturing; Competition). The part read did not contain "12-channel", "Atmos" or "DTS" (about 60k characters at the end of the filing were not read).
- Legacy IMAX film/digital: 6-channel (L/C/R behind screen, "top-center", rear surround clusters). IMAX with Laser adds 6 for 12 total: 4 overhead + 2 extra side surrounds. [SEC-WIKI] — [Wikipedia IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX), Soundtrack / Laser projection.
- Press/venue descriptions: 12 discrete channels plus sub-bass; four ceiling + two side speakers added to existing system; in-theatre microphones/camera auto-calibrate. Secondary press repeating IMAX launch material [MFR via press] — [What Hi-Fi](https://www.whathifi.com/news/imax-launches-laser-projection-system-in-uk-and-it-looks-spectacular), [Pocket-lint](https://www.pocket-lint.com/tv/news/133875-imax-laser-projectors-and-12-channel-surround-sound-to-arrive-in-uk-by-summer), [Box Office Pro, AMC Lincoln Square](https://www.boxofficepro.com/amc-loews-lincoln-square-imax-reopens-next-generation-laser-projection/).
- Forum quote of an interview: IMAX theatres use 5.0, 11.0 or 12.0 layouts with a "roll-off" subwoofer rather than discrete sub. [UNRESOLVED, forum post, unverified; contradicts "12 discrete + sub-bass" press wording] — [Audioholics thread via search](https://forums.audioholics.com/forums/goto/post?id=1517095).
- Home-side only: IMAX Enhanced uses DTS:X; Disney+ IMAX Enhanced titles launched with Dolby Atmos as the only immersive option. This is the home program, not theatre sound. — [Sound & Vision](https://www.soundandvision.com/content/denon-and-marantz-get-imax-enhanced-update), [AVSForum-type thread](https://www.hometheaterforum.com/community/threads/imax-enhanced-dts-x-comes-to-disney.382739/). IMAX 10-K lists IMAX Enhanced as a streaming/consumer program (Item 1, Other Products and Services). [SEC]
- Dolby Atmos cinema: channel-based bed is 9.1; up to 118 audio objects; 128 tracks total; "up to 64 discrete speaker feeds"; up to 64 independently powered speakers; overhead speakers. [MFR] — [Dolby Professional: Atmos cinema](https://professional.dolby.com/cinema/dolby-atmos/).
- Dolby CP950A: up to 64 speaker feeds; up to 64 digital output channels (AES67 or BLU Link); also plays 7.1/5.1. [MFR] — [CP950A brochure](https://professional.dolby.com/siteassets/products/cp950a/dolby_cp950a_digital_brochure.pdf).
- Wikipedia/derived pages call the bed 7.1.2 (10 ch) with 118 objects; conflicts with Dolby's "9.1". Prefer Dolby page. — [Wikipedia Dolby Atmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Atmos).
- DTS:X cinema: GDC Technology said its DTS:X-capable systems reached 1,000 screens worldwide in 2018; B&B Theatres had 29 DTS:X auditoriums in 2019; no current global count found. [MFR/press, dated] — [ETCentric](https://etcentric.org/?p=90721), [Widescreen Review](https://www.widescreenreview.com/news_detail.php?id=21752).

### Inferences
- "IMAX = Atmos" is not supported by the IMAX filing; the safest wording is "proprietary IMAX 5.0/6/12-channel sound". Whether any IMAX auditorium plays Atmos mixes natively is unresolved; Atmos is Dolby's rendering format, and IMAX with Laser's 12-ch mix is channel-based per the sources above (inference).
- IMAX 12 channels (4 overhead) vs Atmos up to 64 independent feeds and 118 objects: Atmos has a much higher format ceiling; real Atmos rooms vary widely in speaker count (inference, no count source found).

### Gaps
- Per-theatre speaker counts for IMAX (any variant) and Dolby Cinema: not found. Sonics and Auro-3D cinema channel specs and install counts: no usable source found. imax.com sound page not read (403). No IMAX statement on "IMAX Immersive" in theatres found.

## (b) Format comparison: projection, brightness, screens

### Takeaway
IMAX 10-K confirms three system families (Laser, Xenon, Film) and a mix of screen sizes; Dolby Cinema = dual Christie 4K 6P laser with Dolby-stated brightness; screen/auditorium size varies and IMAX does not appear to publish per-theatre configs in the filing.

### Cited Findings
- Installed base 12/31/2025: 1,864 IMAX Systems in 91 countries (1,796 commercial multiplexes, 10 commercial destinations, 58 institutional). Table as extracted: Laser 757 (607 in 2024), Xenon 1,089 (1,178), Film 52 (44, excluded from network total); backlog 216 new Laser, 87 new Xenon, 131 Laser upgrades. Extracted columns do not reconcile to 1,864 — verify against original. [SEC] — [IMAX 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/921582/000162828026011770/imax-20251231.htm), Item 1 General / IMAX Network / IMAX Laser Systems.
- 10-K: Laser Systems give "greater brightness and clarity, higher contrast, a wider color gamut and deeper blacks" and are "capable of illuminating the largest screens"; IMAX is developing laser solutions "with various screen sizes" for multiplexes; Film Systems come in "various configurations... and screen sizes". [SEC] (same, Item 1, IMAX Systems; R&D). The 10-K gives no screen dimensions or per-theatre list in the portions read.
- IMAX with Laser (GT): dual 4K, half-pixel offset, 60 fps, brighter than DCI, Rec. 2020; first install Toronto Dec 2014; April 2018 single-unit laser announced to replace xenon on 1.90:1 screens. [SEC-WIKI] — [Wikipedia IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX), Laser projection.
- 15/70 screens 18 x 24 m (59 x 79 ft) typical stated; largest Leonberg 38 x 22 m (2021); digital 1.90:1 screens "much smaller"; smallest IMAX-branded screen ~1/10 area of largest; fewer than a quarter of installs show 70mm film. [SEC-WIKI] — same page, Lead / Theatres / Digital projection.
- Film resolution estimates vary: ~12K (Wikipedia, RedShark), ~18K (another outlet). [EST, disagree] — [RedShark](https://redsharknews.com/technology-computing/item/3601-does-imax-laser-projection-really-compare-with-70mm-film), [SlashGear](https://slashgear.com/2242948/imax-vs-imax-70mm-key-differences-overview/).
- Dolby Cinema: dual Christie 4K 6P laser, custom light path; Dolby brightness "up to 31 fL" 2D Dolby Vision and "up to 14 fL" 3D on unity-gain matte-white screen [MFR] — [Christie/Dolby press release](https://christiedigital.com/press-releases/dolby-selects-christie-to-co-develop-dolby-vision-projection-systems-for-dolby-cinema).
- Wikipedia converts to ~106 cd/m2 (2D) and up to 48 cd/m2 (3D, but "typically 10-14"); notes twin projectors can reach 7,500:1 DCI fixed gamma function, 5,000:1 for non-Dolby Vision content, and "1,000,000:1" for graded content. [SEC-WIKI] — [Wikipedia Dolby Cinema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolby_Cinema), Brightness/Contrast.
- 1,000,000:1: found in Pocket-lint and an Indian review (91mobiles), not in Christie's release (which says contrast "exceeds any other image technology"). Treat as marketing/ratio of black floor; Pocket-lint also cites ~2,500:1 typical cinema and ~8,000:1 for IMAX with Laser (attributed to IMAX's ex-CTO, 2013) [MFR, dated]. — [Pocket-lint](https://www.pocket-lint.com/nl-nl/tv/nieuws/dolby/142028-wat-is-dolby-cinema), [91mobiles](https://www.91mobiles.com/reviews/dolby-cinema-pitch-black-perfection/).
- Film IMAX described as dual or single 15 kW xenon lamps; sources state brightness claims without measurements. [secondary, no measurements] — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2220571/differences-between-imax-70mm/), search results above.
- Seating for Dolby Cinema (recliners): no source retrieved. Standard digital DCI (xenon/laser) figures: DCI spec 14 fL is general knowledge, not sourced here.

### Inferences
- Dolby is brightest/highest-contrast on paper in 2D HDR; IMAX Laser wins on screen size and aspect ratio (1.43 on GT systems). Comparison is apples to oranges because units and bases differ (fL vs cd/m2, sequential vs on/off contrast).
- Neither company publishes per-theatre brightness; real value depends on screen size, gain and lamp/laser output (inference).

### Gaps
- No independent measured data (HDTVTest, Cinema Technology, in70mm, American Cinematographer, Sound & Vision) retrieved. Standard digital DCI 2K/4K details and per-theatre IMAX screen sizes not sourced. IMAX Xenon projector specifics not sourced.

## (c) Myths

### Takeaway
"All IMAX screens are the same" is false by IMAX's own filing (multiple system families, sizes). 1.43:1 applies only to select locations. "LieMAX" is a fan nickname, not a spec.

### Cited Findings
- 10-K: "In select IMAX locations worldwide, movies filmed with IMAX cameras have an IMAX-exclusive 1.43 film aspect ratio" (Item 1, IMAX Film Remastering). [SEC]
- 1.90:1 used by IMAX Digital (2K xenon) and single-unit laser; 1.43:1 shown by 15/70 film, SR and IMAX with Laser (GT). [SEC-WIKI] — [Wikipedia IMAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX), Digital/Laser.
- "LieMAX" coined c.2009 by fans; no technical definition; digital multiplex screens generally smaller. [secondary] — [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/qa-imax-theatre-real-imax-liemax), [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2244674/how-to-tell-difference-imax-liemax/). The "Boston Globe" measurement angle was not found.
- Only a handful of digital IMAX sites in North America can show 1.43:1; 70mm sites reported ~24 US, 9 Canada, 6 elsewhere (undated, secondary). [EST] — [RedShark](https://www.redsharknews.com/studio-broadcast/item/3550-bigger-is-better-imax-70mm-film-projection-present-and-future) via search summary.
- "More pixels always better": no source on this directly; related data above show Dolby 4K dual laser has similar nominal resolution to IMAX Laser 4K while differing in brightness, screen size and sound. [Inference]

### Gaps
- No primary IMAX page defining "IMAX with Laser" screen minimums (one outlet says designed for screens over 75 ft, unverified).
